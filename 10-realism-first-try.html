Workflow

Step by step
  1. One brief: four characters, locations, props, camera, voice-over.
  2. Project folder; character sheets at 2K.
  3. Locations in Soul Cinema; LEGO prop sheets.
  4. Three reusable scene structures; draft 480p then 1080p.
  5. Convert characters to minifigure sheets for the finale.
  6. Voice-over inside the platform; cut to the beat.
